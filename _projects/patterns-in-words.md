title: Patterns in words

The Erdős–Szekeres theorem guarantees a long monotone subsequence in any long enough sequence of distinct numbers. We prove an analogue for words on \\(\mathbb{N}\\) with repeated values, where a repeat is any occurrence of a value after its first: every word with \\(k \cdot n^6 + 1\\) repeats contains one of seven patterns, and for \\(k = 1\\) we construct a word with \\(n^6\\) repeats that avoids them all, so the bound is tight. The project began at the pre-conference workshop of Permutation Patterns 2025 in St Andrews.
