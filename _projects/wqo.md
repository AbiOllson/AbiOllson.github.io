---
title: Well-quasi-ordering of Cayley permutations
short: wqo
authors: [bean, ironmonger, ollson]
order: 4
---
A partially ordered set is well-quasi-ordered if it contains no infinite antichain and no infinite strictly decreasing sequence. The set of all Cayley permutations is partially ordered by pattern containment, here under the consecutive order, where an occurrence of a pattern must use adjacent letters. In this project we characterise the sets of pattern-avoiding Cayley permutations that are well-quasi-ordered under the consecutive order.
