A collection of Python libraries for working with Cayley permutations: creating them and their avoidance classes, checking containment, generating and counting classes by brute force, and finding generating functions with an adaptation of Combinatorial Exploration and the tilescope algorithm to Cayley permutations. It accompanies the [combinatorial exploration](/projects/combinatorial-exploration/) project. [All contributors are listed on GitHub](https://github.com/AbiOllson/CayleyPerms/graphs/contributors).
