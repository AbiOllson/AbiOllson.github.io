A Python library for enumerating Cayley permutations, restricted growth functions and restricted growth functions of perfect matchings with the vertical and horizontal insertion encodings. Given a basis of Cayley permutations, it checks whether the class has a regular insertion encoding of either type and, if so, finds a specification and its rational generating function. It accompanies the papers in the research project [The insertion encoding](/projects/insertion-encodings/).
