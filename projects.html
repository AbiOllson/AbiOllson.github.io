Cayley permutations

A Cayley permutation is a word on \(\mathbb{N}\) in which every value from 1 to the maximum appears at least once. They are in bijection with ordered set partitions. Many combinatorial objects can be defined as words on \(\mathbb{N}\) such as set partitions, permutations, inversion sequences, restricted growth functions and ascent sequences. Standardising a subsequence of a word by replacing each occurrence of the smallest value by 1, the next smallest by 2, and so on, gives a Cayley permutation, so Cayley permutations are a natural way to define pattern avoidance in words on \(\mathbb{N}\).

The focus of my research so far has been the avoidance of Cayley permutations in combinatorial objects defined as words on \(\mathbb{N}\).
